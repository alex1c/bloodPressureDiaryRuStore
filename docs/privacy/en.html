Privacy Policy

App “Blood Pressure Diary” / «Дневник давления» (com.calculatorplatform.bpdiary)
Effective date: 6 October 2026

RU EN ES DE

1. Overview

Blood Pressure Diary is a mobile app for keeping a personal diary of blood pressure, pulse, and related health metrics that you enter yourself. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions, and does not replace consulting a doctor.

2. Data you enter

You may store locally on the device:

  • blood pressure (systolic and diastolic);
  • pulse;
  • notes and tags;
  • health metrics (weight, glucose, SpO₂, temperature);
  • medications, schedules, and intake check-offs;
  • family profile names;
  • language and reminder settings;
  • backup files you create manually.

3. Where data is stored

Primary health diary data is stored locally on your device in an SQLite database inside the app.

There is no developer cloud account, no developer backend sync, and no central user health database operated by the developer.

4. Android Auto Backup

Android Auto Backup for this app’s data is disabled. Measurement databases and related settings are not intended for automatic upload to Google cloud backup.

The supported transfer path is a user-initiated JSON backup from Settings, shared via the Android Share Sheet or restored via the system document picker.

5. Profiles

Family profile names stay on your device and only separate records inside the app.

6. JSON backup

Backups are created only when you explicitly export them. The app does not upload backup files to a developer server.

7. Doctor PDF report

PDFs are generated on-device. Sharing happens only when you use the system Share Sheet.

8. Notifications

The app uses local notifications for measurement and medication reminders. There is no developer remote push service.

9. Deleting your data

Inside the app you can delete:

  • individual blood-pressure readings;
  • health metric entries;
  • medications and intake records;
  • reminders;
  • family profiles (except the last remaining profile).

There are no user accounts and no developer-hosted health database. Deleting local records (or uninstalling the app) removes diary data from the app storage. Technical data already sent by analytics or ads SDKs is handled under those vendors’ policies.

10. AppMetrica analytics

Technical usage analytics use Yandex AppMetrica.

We do not send health values in analytics events:

  • blood pressure or pulse numbers;
  • weight, glucose, SpO₂, or temperature values;
  • medication names or dosages;
  • notes or profile names.

Events describe feature usage only (for example, that a reading was created). The SDK may process technical device identifiers per AppMetrica’s policy.

11. Advertising and Advertising ID

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads. The SDK may process technical device information and the Advertising ID to serve and measure ads, subject to Yandex policies and device settings.

12. Internet access

Internet access is used for ads and AppMetrica events. The diary core works offline.
