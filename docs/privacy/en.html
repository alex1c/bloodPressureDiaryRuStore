1. Developer and contact

This app is developed and published by an individual developer reachable at rustore-alex1c@yandex.ru (privacy and support).

This policy describes how the app and its optional third-party SDKs handle information. It is not legal advice and does not create guarantees beyond what is stated here.

2. Purpose of the app

Blood Pressure Diary helps you keep a personal diary of blood pressure, pulse, medications, and related health metrics that you enter yourself. It is a consumer diary tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions, and does not replace professional medical advice.

3. Health records you keep locally

You may store on your device, among other things:

  • blood pressure (systolic / diastolic) and pulse;
  • notes and tags for measurements;
  • additional metrics (for example weight, glucose, SpO₂, temperature);
  • medications, schedules, and intake check-offs;
  • family profile display names;
  • language and local reminder settings.

These diary values are entered by you and are intended for on-device use.

4. No account and no developer cloud diary

The app does not require a user account. There is no developer-operated cloud sync server for your health diary and no central database of user measurements maintained by the developer.

5. Local SQLite storage and user exports

Primary diary data is stored in a local SQLite database inside the app sandbox on your device. Android Auto Backup for this app’s data is disabled, so the diary is not intended for automatic upload to Google cloud backup.

You may create a JSON backup or a PDF doctor report on the device. Those files leave the device only if you explicitly share them through the Android Share Sheet or similar system UI. The developer does not receive those files automatically. You remain responsible for any destination you choose.

6. Yandex Mobile Ads

If you allow advertising, the app uses Yandex Mobile Ads to show banners and occasional interstitial ads. Ad units for RuStore and Google Play are configured separately. Google AdMob is not included in this release.

According to Yandex Mobile Ads documentation for Google Play Data Safety, the SDK may process device or other identifiers (including Advertising ID when permission allows) for advertising and marketing purposes, and encrypts listed data in transit over HTTPS/TLS. Default vendor guidance does not treat health diary content as collected by the Ads SDK. See Yandex’s current Data Safety guide for Mobile Ads.

Ads are not shown over critical measurement entry flows or system permission dialogs. This integration does not provide a separate guaranteed “non-personalized ads only” mode: turning advertising off blocks ad requests.

7. AppMetrica (analytics)

If you allow technical analytics, the app uses Yandex AppMetrica to measure app usage. First-party code sends only allowlisted technical events (for example opening Graphs or changing locale codes). It does not send blood pressure or pulse values, medication names, doses, intake status, health metric values, notes, tags, or profile names.

AppMetrica’s own Google Play Data Safety guidance indicates that, in its default configuration, the SDK may process crash-related and diagnostic / performance information and device or other IDs (when permitted), and encrypts listed data in transit. Location tracking is disabled in this app’s configuration. Exact categories depend on the shipped SDK build and vendor documentation.

8. Advertising ID and device information

When optional SDKs are allowed, they may process technical device information and the Google Advertising ID (GAID), subject to Android settings (including ads personalization / reset ID). The app’s TypeScript code does not read the Advertising ID directly; the Ads SDK declares the AD_ID permission as documented by Yandex for SDK 4.5.0+.

10. Consent, refusal, and withdrawal

On launch (and again from Settings), you can allow or refuse Technical analytics (AppMetrica) and Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads) independently. Core diary features remain available if you refuse.

Choices for optional SDKs apply after you confirm them in the app for the current session. You can change them later in Settings. Refusal and withdrawal stop first-party enabling of the corresponding SDK paths; already-transmitted technical data held by Yandex continues under Yandex’s policies.

For Yandex Mobile Ads, the app passes your advertising purpose decision via the SDK setUserConsent API as described in Yandex GDPR documentation, and does not call ads initialization until advertising is allowed. For AppMetrica, activation and event sending are gated behind analytics permission in app code.

This in-app dialog is a first-party purpose gate for Yandex SDKs. It is not a Google-certified CMP / IAB TCF consent tool. Google’s certified-CMP requirements for Google advertising demand apply when Google AdMob / Google demand is used; this release does not ship AdMob.

The app is distributed internationally (including via Google Play closed testing). If you enable optional Yandex SDKs, related technical data may be processed by Yandex according to Yandex’s terms and privacy notices, which may involve processing outside your country of residence.

This policy does not invent specific hosting countries, SCCs, or EU representative details. See Yandex’s published terms for AppMetrica and Mobile Ads.

12. Retention

Local diary data remains on your device until you delete records or uninstall the app.

Technical data processed by Yandex SDKs (if enabled) is retained according to Yandex’s policies and product settings. The developer does not operate a separate server-side retention schedule for that data and cannot truthfully state a single fixed retention period for all Yandex systems.

13. Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on applicable law (for example GDPR / UK GDPR where they apply), you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

For the local diary, you can edit or delete records in the app. For questions about this app’s privacy practices, email rustore-alex1c@yandex.ru. Requests about data held solely by Yandex should also follow Yandex’s published privacy channels where available.

14. Deletion and limits of developer control

You can delete individual measurements, health metrics, medications, reminders, and profiles (subject to keeping at least one profile), or uninstall the app to remove local storage.

The developer cannot guarantee deletion of technical data already processed by Yandex Mobile Ads or AppMetrica. Those services follow their own deletion and retention rules.

15. Security

Local diary data stays in the app sandbox. Optional SDK traffic uses the encryption practices described by the respective vendors (for example HTTPS/TLS for Mobile Ads Data Safety disclosures; AppMetrica documents encryption in transit for listed data types). No security measure is perfect; protect your device with a screen lock and share exports only with trusted recipients.

16. Age

The app is intended for adults managing personal or family health diaries. It is not directed at children and is not designed for Google Play’s Families / Designed for Families programs. If you are under the digital consent age in your country, use the app only with a parent or guardian as required by local law.
