## Full description

Blood Pressure Diary helps you keep a personal log of blood pressure, pulse, and related health metrics you enter yourself.

You can:
• save blood pressure and pulse readings with notes and tags;
• review history and charts for a chosen period;
• track medications with local reminders;
• log weight, glucose, SpO₂, and temperature;
• use multiple family profiles on one device;
• create a PDF summary and share it via the system Share Sheet;
• export a manual JSON backup of your diary.

Diary data stays on your device. There is no developer account and no cloud sync of your health journal.

The app shows ads (Yandex Mobile Ads) and uses AppMetrica for technical analytics without sending blood pressure values, pulse values, medication names, or notes.

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not replace consulting a doctor.
